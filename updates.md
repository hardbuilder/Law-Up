

## ✅ Changes Made

### 1. **Structured Chat Responses**
- **Before**: Long, unstructured paragraphs
- **After**: Organized bullet points with clear sections:
  - **Key Points**: Main information
  - **Recommendations**: Actionable advice  
  - **Note**: Legal disclaimer

### 2. **Concise Length Limits**
- Chat responses limited to **200 words max**
- Document analysis items limited to **25-50 words each**
- Automatic truncation at 1000 characters if needed

### 3. **Document Analysis Structure**
- **Summary**: 1-2 sentences only
- **Key Clauses**: Max 40 words per clause
- **Risks**: Max 40 words + specific recommendations (30 words)
- **Benefits**: Max 30 words each
- **Obligations/Rights**: Max 25 words each

### 4. **User-Friendly Format**
- Clear **markdown formatting** with headers
- **Bullet points** for easy scanning
- **Simple language** instead of legal jargon
- **Actionable insights** rather than general information

### 5. **Technical Improvements**
- Fixed file upload validation issues
- Better error handling and logging
- Enhanced CORS and file type detection

## 🎯 Result

The AI now provides:
- ✅ **Shorter responses** (under 200 words)
- ✅ **Clear structure** with bullet points
- ✅ **Actionable advice** instead of theory
- ✅ **Easy to scan** format
