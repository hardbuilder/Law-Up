EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on January 1, 2024, between ABC Company ("Company") and John Doe ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee shall serve as Software Engineer and shall perform duties as assigned by the Company.

2. COMPENSATION
Employee shall receive a salary of $80,000 per year, payable in accordance with Company's standard payroll practices.

3. BENEFITS
Employee shall be entitled to participate in Company's standard benefit programs including health insurance and 401(k) plan.

4. TERMINATION
This agreement may be terminated by either party with 30 days written notice. Company may terminate for cause immediately.

5. NON-COMPETE
Employee agrees not to compete with Company for a period of 2 years after termination within a 50-mile radius.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all Company proprietary information.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product created during employment shall be the property of the Company.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
